How to read the backtest

Use proper scoring rules first, then check accuracy and calibration.

  • Brier score: evaluates probability forecasts; lower is better.
  • Log loss: strongly penalises confident predictions that are wrong; lower is better.
  • Outcome accuracy: the share of correctly predicted outcomes. It ignores how well-calibrated the probabilities were.
  • Weekly frozen ratings: every match in a week is predicted before any result from that week changes ratings.
  • Validation versus final test: tune parameters on validation seasons, then use later untouched seasons for the final comparison. Never choose a formula because it won on the final test.

Historical results are evidence for the tested competition and time period, not proof of universal superiority. Keep the live rating system unchanged until a candidate wins across multiple chronological test windows and preferably another competition.
