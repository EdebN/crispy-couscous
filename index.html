Experiment notes

What the numbers mean, because tables alone are not science.

  • Brier score: measures the accuracy of predicted outcomes.
  • Log loss: penalises confidently incorrect predictions.
  • Rank correlation: measures how closely the ladder follows true simulated skill.
  • Mean swing: measures rating movement between periods. Lower is not automatically better.
  • Objective: 45% Brier score, 35% log loss, and 20% ranking error. On finalists, seed-to-seed variation is penalised; rating movement is reported but not blindly minimised.
  • Robust objective: for holdout-tested finalists, the mean objective is increased by 0.25 × its across-seed standard deviation. The baseline is exactly 1.0. Untested rows show the screening objective.

Simulation results are not proof of real-world performance. The broad sweep uses one set of simulations; shortlisted candidates are compared on independent holdout seeds. No simulated result automatically changes the live rating system. Historical match data is still the final test.
