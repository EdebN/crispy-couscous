Experiment notes

What the numbers mean, because tables alone are not science.

  • Brier score: measures the accuracy of predicted outcomes.
  • Log loss: penalises confidently incorrect predictions.
  • Rank correlation: measures how closely the ladder follows true simulated skill.
  • Mean swing: measures rating movement between periods. Lower is not automatically better.
  • Objective: a weighted comparison score, not a probability or a rating.

Simulation results are not proof of real-world performance. Validate the finalists against historical competition results and previously unused random seeds before changing the live system.
